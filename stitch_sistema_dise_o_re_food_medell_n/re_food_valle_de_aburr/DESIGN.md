## Brand & Style

### Personality & Mission
The design system positions food recovery not as an ad-hoc charity effort, but as high-efficiency civic and ecological infrastructure. The tone blends editorial poise, technological rigor, and civic urgency. It communicates operational excellence, environmental accountability, and dignity for donors, logistics operators, and beneficiary institutions across the Aburrá Valley.

### Target Audience
- **Commercial & Agricultural Donors:** Supermarkets, hotels, restaurants, and logistics hubs in Medellín, Envigado, Itagüí, Sabaneta, and Bello requiring rapid dispatch, tax receipts, and ESG metrics.
- **Logistics & Recovery Teams:** Field operators tracking weight (kg/tons), perishable life cycles, and cold-chain status on mobile interfaces under direct sunlight.
- **Social Foundations & Community Kitchens:** Beneficiary administrators managing food receipt, distribution logs, and nutritional balance.

### Emotional Response
Trust, urgency without panic, modern ecological stewardship, institutional durability, and human warmth rooted in local pride.

### Aesthetic Style: Editorial Tech Minimal
The design fuses high-end editorial composition (deliberate whitespace, asymmetrical typographic tension, and warm ivory planes) with the precision of a high-performance logistics SaaS. Visual weight is handled through deep, organic forest greens contrasted sharply against energetic lime accents, avoiding sterile corporate grays and paternalistic NGO tropes.

## Colors

### Core Palette Application
- **Primary Deep (`#123D32`):** Grounding color for institutional layouts, primary buttons, major headlines, and high-impact metrics.
- **Primary Dark (`#17483B`):** Hover states, primary interactive variants, and deep contrasting surfaces.
- **Accent High-Energy (`#D9F27A`):** Focal highlights, actionable status badges, rescue countdown tags, and progress fill bars against dark green backgrounds.
- **Accent Vibrant (`#A9D84B`):** Positive metric trends, completed delivery states, and supporting biological markers.
- **Canvas Warm Neutral (`#F7F7F0`):** The ivory base canvas, removing clinical glare while preserving high contrast against pure white cards.
- **Card / Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority modules, elevated cards, and transactional data blocks.
- **Charcoal Forest (`#202D29`):** Primary body copy, table figures, and active navigation items.
- **Muted Forest Slate (`#77817A`):** Secondary metadata, unit markers (`kg`, `t`), timestamps, and inactive iconography.
- **Subtle Border (`#E5E7DE`):** Structural 1px separation lines across cards, table cells, and dividers.

### Functional & Semantic States
- **Critical / Urgent (`#E27D60`):** Food expiration windows under 2 hours, broken cold-chain warnings, and failed pickups.
- **Success / Rescued (`#123D32` text on `#A9D84B` / `#D9F27A` container):** Completed redistribution, verified kilograms received, and signed receipts.
- **Pending / Neutral (`#77817A` text on `#E5E7DE` container):** Scheduled pickups and queued verification logs.

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchy
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** is designated exclusively for titles, display numbers, impact figures, and section leads. Its tight geometry and modern cuts establish editorial authority and high confidence.
- **Inter** handles all continuous prose, operational forms, table listings, and data entries. Inter's optical neutrality prevents eye fatigue during intensive field audits.

### Numeric & Data Formatting
All technical metrics (e.g., `1.840 kg`, `COP $12.450.000`, `08:30 AM`) must use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure alignment across recurring rows and inventory audit tables.

### Editorial Treatments
Display headlines should pair tight line-heights with slight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). Never use uppercase transformation for titles; reserve all-caps strictly for small status labels (`label-sm`) with wide tracking (`0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, max content constraint of 1440px, 48px canvas margins, and 24px column gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid grid, 32px canvas margins, and 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, 20px canvas margins, and 16px gutters.

### 8px Modular Rhythm
Layout distances follow strict multiples of 8px (`0.5rem`), with a single 4px step (`space-xs`) reserved for tight internal component relationships (such as icon-to-label gaps and compact data badges).

### Regional Card Composition
Logistics and metric cards prioritize horizontal content pairs on desktop (e.g., metric number aligned left, sparkline or regional municipality tag aligned right), collapsing to vertical stacks on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Grounded Flatness
The design system rejects deep drop shadows and multi-layered skeuomorphism in favor of clear planes, crisp boundary definitions, and subtle elevation changes. 

### Surface Tiers
1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** Warm Ivory canvas (`#F7F7F0`).
2. **Structural Layer (Level 1):** Solid white card containers (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border (`#E5E7DE`). No drop shadow is applied in default states.
3. **Floating & Interactive Layer (Level 2):** Applied exclusively to floating action bars, active pick-up modals, and hovered route cards:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.04);`
   - Shadow colors are explicitly tinted with the primary forest hue (`#123D32`) rather than pure black or gray, maintaining warmth across the ivory background.

### Dividers & Overlays
Dividers must never exceed 1px and must strictly use `#E5E7DE`. Dialog backdrops utilize `#123D32` at 40% opacity with a 4px blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) to keep user focus pinned on the interactive modal.

## Shapes

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Cards, Modals & Data Panels:** `12px` (`0.75rem`) to `16px` (`1rem`) corner radius. This balances industrial structure with contemporary warmth.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs & Selects:** Fixed `10px` (`0.625rem`) radius for crisp, ergonomic touch targets.
- **Chips, Pills, & Municipality Indicators:** Fully rounded `9999px` radius (`pill-shaped`), establishing immediate visual distinction from actionable rectangular buttons.
- **Nested Elements:** Inner elements must follow proportional radius constraints (`outer_radius - padding`), preventing awkward corner gaps inside interactive containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#123D32` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `10px` radius, 48px height (`h-12`) for touch accessibility. Hover shifts to `#17483B`. Active press triggers a subtle `scale(0.99)`.
- **High-Energy Accent (Urgent / Immediate Rescue):** Solid `#D9F27A` background, `#123D32` text, `10px` radius, font weight 600. Reserved for high-value operations like "Confirmar Rescate Inmediato". Hover shifts to `#A9D84B`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border 1px solid `#E5E7DE`, text `#202D29`. Hover changes surface to `#FFFFFF` with border color `#77817A`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E27D60`, text `#E27D60`. Hover triggers filled background `#E27D60` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Geometry:** Height 24px, padding 0 10px, radius `9999px`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Urgente / Crítico (< 2h para rescate):** Background `#FDF0ED`, text `#E27D60`, 1px border `#F9D5CC`.
- **Rescatado / Completado:** Background `#F2F9E8`, text `#123D32`, 1px border `#A9D84B`.
- **En Tránsito:** Background `#F2F6F5`, text `#17483B`, 1px border `#123D32` at 20% opacity.
- **Territorio (Valle de Aburrá):** Pill badge with `#FFFFFF` surface, `#77817A` text, 1px `#E5E7DE` border (e.g., `Medellín · El Poblado`, `Itagüí · Centro de Acopio`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E5E7DE`, radius `10px`, font size 15px (`body-md`), text `#202D29`. Placeholder styled in `#77817A`. Focus ring applies a 1.5px border in `#123D32` with no loud glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size 20x20px, radius 4px (checkbox) or circular (radio), border 1.5px solid `#77817A`. Checked state transitions to `#123D32` background with `#D9F27A` check icon.

### Data & Metric Cards
- **Structure:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E5E7DE`, 16px corner radius, padding 24px (`space-lg`).
- **Hierarchy:** Top row contains secondary metric label (`label-lg`, `#77817A`) and an optional status pill. Middle row houses the primary measurement in `metric-display` (`#123D32`) paired with inline unit labels (`kg`, `Ton`, `Raciones`). Bottom row indicates comparative time frames or territorial coverage in `body-sm`.

### Lists & Audit Tables
- **Rows:** Minimum height 56px, alternating subtle ivory-tint hover states (`rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.02)`), separated by 1px solid `#E5E7DE`.
- **Headers:** Typography `label-md`, uppercase, tracking `0.04em`, text `#77817A`.
- **Cells:** Left-aligned strings; right-aligned numerical quantities, weights, and time stamps.

### Specialized Component: Route & Perishability Indicator
A custom compound component designed for field monitors displaying:
- Time remaining until food deterioration (progress bar using `#D9F27A` transitioning to `#E27D60` below 25%).
- Route chain tag: Origin node (e.g., `Éxito Envigado`) → Destination node (e.g., `Comedor Comunitario Bello`).
